## Product identity (confirmed 2026-10-12)
- **CohortData** is the company. **CohortGlobal** is the product: a data-collection platform run by CohortData for its external vendors, agencies and freelancers.
- CohortData has **no sister companies**. The organizations on the platform are CohortData (parent) and agencies.

## Build phases
1. **Part 1 — Platform foundation:** identity, organizations, roles, invitations, projects, applications, contracts, agency approval, member assignment, portals and dashboards.
2. **Part 2 — Data collection:** public links, sessions, submissions, metadata validation, QC, delivery, storage.

## Operating countries
Vendors and members come mainly from **Singapore, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Indonesia, Brazil and India**. The platform must be a production-grade, official application.

- **Languages (Q9):** English only at launch. The architecture is translation-ready (English message catalogue, direction-neutral layouts); no other languages are built in v1.
- **Personal data:** UTC timestamps shown in the user's timezone; phone numbers in E.164; ISO country codes; names in any script.
- **Privacy law:** DPDP India, PDPA Singapore, PDPL Saudi, PDPL UAE, PDP Law Indonesia, LGPD Brazil. Face and voice data are sensitive or biometric. Cross-border transfer review is postponed (Q14 / #41).
- **ID documents:** store document type and country; avoid raw national ID numbers unless required.
- **Low bandwidth:** mobile-first pages; resumable uploads in Part 2.

## Confirmed rules

### People and organizations
- **Agency participants (Q2):**
  - Agency Owner, Agency Manager and Agency Member have login accounts and an agency membership.
  - Agency Members are the agency's employees, invited only by their own agency.
  - **Agency Freelancers have no account.** They only receive a public submission link generated by the agency, fill in the form and submit.
- **One Owner and one Manager per agency (#5, #8):** an agency has exactly one Owner and at most one Manager.
- **Owner vs Manager (Q3, #5–#7):** the same powers, except:
  - only the Owner can invite or remove the Manager;
  - the Manager can never remove the Owner;
  - only the Owner signs contracts (#24) and requests deactivation or ownership transfer (#9, #10).
- **Agency profile edits (#7):** the agency cannot edit its company profile or documents directly. It submits a **change request**, which the Super Admin approves or rejects. (Applied to both Owner and Manager; see implementation decision ID-07.)
- **Super Admin powers over agencies (#6, #11):** the Super Admin can invite, edit and remove agency people and can edit or remove approved agencies from the agency list.
- **Ownership transfer (#9):** allowed, carefully. The Owner requests a transfer to the agency's Manager and the Super Admin approves it; the Super Admin can also reassign ownership if the Owner is gone.
- **Agency deactivation (#10):** the Owner can request deactivation; only the Super Admin deactivates.
- **Agency Members (#1–#4):**
  - see only the projects assigned to them by the agency;
  - see only their own submissions; the Manager (and Owner) see everything in the agency;
  - cannot create public links;
  - see only their own member dashboard, not the agency dashboard.
- **One org per user:** a user belongs to at most one organization.
- **Direct freelancer → agency:** no conversion. The direct account is deleted and the person signs up again through the agency invitation.
- **No Team level (Q8).**

### Parent team
- **Roles in v1 (Q7):** Super Admin, **Operations Manager** (added 2026-10-19), Project Manager, QC Lead, QC Reviewer. The Finance role is deferred.
- **Operations Manager (Q32, 2026-10-19):** below the Super Admin and above Project Managers, parent team only. Assigns Project Managers to projects. A Project Manager can be assigned to one or more projects and **sees only the projects assigned to them** (e.g. PM 1 → 2 projects, PM 2 → 1, PM 3 → 1). Agencies are not affected.
- **One Super Admin (#16, #17):** there is exactly one Super Admin for now.
- **First Super Admin (Q5):** a secure, one-time bootstrap (`docs/architecture.md` section 6.5).
- **Staff invitations (Q5):** only the Super Admin invites CohortData staff.
- **QC staff:** QC Lead and QC Reviewer are parent-team roles only.
- **QC visibility (#43):** Project Managers can see the QC queue (of their projects).
- **QC levels (Q32):** one review level in v1. A Level 2 spot-check can be added later.

### Agencies
- **Agency approval (Q4):** the Super Admin approves or rejects agencies that sign up.
- **Agency invitations (Q4, #15):** only the Super Admin invites agencies. **An invited agency is approved automatically (#11).**
- **Verification details (#12):** legal company name, country, registration or tax number, contact person, contact email and phone, company website, and company documents. The list can be extended later.
- **Re-applying (#13):** a rejected agency can apply again **after 3 days**.
- **Suspension (#14):** the Super Admin can suspend and later reactivate an approved agency.
- **Invitation validity (#18):** invitations (staff, agency, manager, member) are valid for **7 days** and can be re-sent.

### Projects and applications
- **Project approval:** approving an agency for a project is enough; the agency decides internally who works.
- **Job page in v1 (Q6):** application questions, a contract signed after approval, and an applicant-type tag (Agency / Individual / Both).
- **Project invitations (#19):** the parent team can invite specific agencies to a project, and agencies can also apply on their own. Both end in a normal application that is approved or rejected.
- **Applicant-type tag (#20, assistant suggestion accepted by default):** it can change only while the project has no applications.
- **Question types (#21, suggestion accepted by default):** short text, long text, single choice, multiple choice, yes/no. No file upload.
- **Editing questions (#22):** questions are versioned. Once applications exist, only the Super Admin can change them, which creates a new version.
- **Editing answers (#23):** applicants cannot edit answers after submitting.

### Contracts
- **Signer (#24):** the Agency Owner signs for an agency.
- **Direct freelancers (#25):** sign their own contract after approval.
- **Method (#26):** click-to-accept for v1 (typed name, time, IP and browser recorded).
- **Template (#27):** one contract template per project, customized per project (for example, the face-data contract).
- **Countersigning (#28):** CohortData countersigns.
- **Access gate (#29):** an approved applicant can access the project, assign members or create links **only after both signatures**.

### Audit
- **Platform-wide audit (#30):** Super Admin only.
- **Agency audit (#31):** agencies see their own audit history only.

### Collection (Part 2)
- **No email code (#32):** no-login freelancers do not confirm their email.
- **Remarks (#33):** freelancers see rejection remarks on the link page only; no email.
- **One freelancer per link (#34).**
- **Submission limits (#35):** set per project.
- **Revocation (#36):** the Super Admin can revoke an agency's link (as well as the agency).
- **Reopened session (#37):** a fresh 5 hours, capped at link expiry.
- **Duplicates (#38):** auto-reject on the same file hash, normalized email, phone number or ID number. Send to QC on the same name and date of birth, the same device under different names, or the same name and phone with a different email.
- **Upload size (#39):** configured per project by the Project Manager.
- **Metadata (#40):** flexible JSON per project, with key fields indexed.
- **Legal review of storage location (#41):** postponed.
- **Notifications (#42):** email only; no WhatsApp in v1.
- **Links:** expire 24 hours after creation; sessions last 5 hours.
- **Consent / eKYC:** configured per project.
- **Storage:** the company's 2 TB server; details in Part 2.
- **QC flow:** metadata validation → QC queue → QC Lead assigns → QC Reviewer decides; a rejection carries a remark.

### Collection answers (2026-10-19)
- **First real brief (Q34):** the AIxBlock face-video job (owner's screenshots): a notice about agency payment; instructions and a video; full name and email; profile options (gender, age group, nationality, skin tone, background, light condition, device, accessory) limited by quotas ("Only these options are available for your demographic"); guides with example images, a video and a description for chosen options (e.g. Indoor + Mixed Lighting, Skin tone Medium, Accessory None); **Start Session**, then a **5-hour window**; biometric consent with a checkbox and a drawn or typed signature; one video recorded with the phone camera (no WhatsApp); eKYC with ID front (required) and back (optional), JPEG/PNG up to 30 MB; Cancel Session / Upload Video.
- **Supabase plan (Q33):** stay on Free for testing with 3–4 MB videos (limits: 50 MB per file, 1 GB total).
- **Video checks (Q35):** length checked in the browser now; a server check later.
- **Reviewers see personal details (Q36):** shown by default; the PM chooses which fields QC can see.
- **Rejected files (Q37):** deleted after 30 days; the record and metadata are kept.
- **Delivery (Q38):** CSV manifest (with the files) for now.
- **Delivery batches (Q39):** created by PMs and the Super Admin.
- **Agency Manager (Q40):** sees the videos and status of their own agency's submissions (members and freelancers), nothing from other agencies.
- **Reviewer assignment (Q41):** the QC Lead assigns for now; reviewers picking from the queue may be added later.
